Safety and scope

General fitness education, not medical care

Calisthenics.Coach teaches bodyweight exercise patterns and organises general fitness sessions. It does not diagnose injury, assess medical readiness, prescribe rehabilitation or replace a qualified clinician, teacher, coach or responsible adult.

Use the app conservatively

  • Use a clear, non-slip area and stable supports.
  • Choose a regression whenever the intended movement cannot be controlled.
  • Stop for pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, faintness or unusual shortness of breath.
  • Do not practise advanced inversions, jumps or unstable-support exercises without appropriate instruction and surroundings.
  • People with a health condition, recent injury, pregnancy or major concern should seek individual professional guidance.

Tracking labels

“Watch experimental” means a future wearable detector may be feasible; it does not mean the web app currently counts the movement automatically. All workouts remain manually controllable.
