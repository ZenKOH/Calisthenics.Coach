Training principles

Learn

The app favours clear progressions, balanced movement and controllable training over novelty.

01

Consistency first

A manageable programme repeated over time is more useful than a complicated session that cannot be sustained.

02

Progress one variable

Improve technique, then repetitions, duration, sets, range, tempo or leverage. Avoid changing every variable at once.

03

Balance patterns

Use lower-body, push, pull or posterior-shoulder, trunk, conditioning, mobility and balance work across the week.

04

Keep control

Stop a set before movement quality collapses. Training to failure is not required for general fitness progress.

05

Use regressions

A regression is not failure. It is a way to practise the intended pattern with an appropriate level of load and control.

06

Respect scope

This app provides general exercise education, not diagnosis, injury assessment, physiotherapy or emergency advice.

Programme basis

The default templates emphasise regular muscle-strengthening, full-body movement coverage, scalable bodyweight work and progressive difficulty. They deliberately avoid appearance goals, calorie targets and extreme-volume challenges.
