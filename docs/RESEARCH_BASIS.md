# Research Basis

The implementation reflects the following current source categories:

- GitHub Pages custom workflows and official Pages actions.
- MDN guidance on installable Progressive Web Apps, manifests and service workers.
- WHO physical-activity guidance supporting regular movement, major-muscle-group strengthening and balance work.
- ACSM's 2026 resistance-training update emphasising consistency, individualised volume and the effectiveness of bodyweight and home-based resistance training.

Primary sources:

- https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/using-custom-workflows-with-github-pages
- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Progressive_web_apps/Guides/Making_PWAs_installable
- https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity
- https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/

The app does not reproduce a clinical protocol. Exercise descriptions are original educational summaries and require qualified review before use in clinical, school or regulated contexts.
