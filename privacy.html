Privacy

Local-first by design

Calisthenics.Coach does not require an account, analytics identifier, advertising profile or cloud database. Workout history, preferences and custom programmes are stored in your browser using IndexedDB.

What the app stores

  • Completed workout summaries
  • Custom programmes
  • Voice, sound and reduced-motion preferences

What the app does not collect

  • No camera or microphone data
  • No GPS location
  • No health records
  • No body measurements
  • No advertising identifiers

You can export a backup, import it on another browser, or delete all local data from Settings. Hosting infrastructure may retain ordinary web-server request logs according to GitHub’s policies.
